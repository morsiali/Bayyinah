/ The problem[ 01 / 07 ]

A payment request is easy to fake. A decision is hard to defend.

Supplier emails get spoofed, bank details get “updated”, and the person who paid is left explaining why. Most teams have no record of what was checked, by whom, or against what.

The decision ledger

Every compared detail lands in exactly one column

Matches the reference

The detail agrees with the supplier record you hold. Recorded — but a match never proves a request is genuine.

Differs from the reference

Account, name, sender domain or amount disagrees. Quoted from the source, and it makes independent verification mandatory.

Not yet verified

Nobody has confirmed it through a trusted channel. On most requests this is the column that matters most.

With Bayyinah

Every approval carries its evidence.

The request, the quotes behind each indicator, the comparison with your reference, the verification call and the reviewer’s rationale — kept together as one Decision Passport you can print or show an auditor.

/ Rules of engagement[ 03 / 07 ]

Fully assisted. Entirely under your control.

Preparers build cases, reviewers decide, administrators keep supplier references honest. Each person signs in with their own account; the company is never a shared login.

Reads and quotes

Extracts amount, beneficiary, account and sender, and quotes the exact words behind every indicator. Unsupported quotes are discarded.

Flags, never judges

Urgency, secrecy, requests to bypass procedure and new contact details are reasons to check — never a verdict, never a fraud score.

Bounded agent

Seven allow-listed tools. It prepares the case and creates the verification task — it cannot approve, close, contact anyone or edit supplier details.

Full audit trail

Every analysis, correction, tool call, verification and decision is recorded with who, when and on which evidence version.

/ Request types[ 06 / 07 ]

Three request types. Each with its own rules.

Invoice payment

Compares the invoice with the supplier reference. Verification is required as soon as the account, beneficiary, amount or sender differs, or the wording asks to bypass procedure.

Rules: account · name · domain · amount

Bank account change

Always requires a recorded independent verification through a reference channel and a reviewer who is not the case creator. The AI cannot waive either.

Always verified · separate reviewer

New beneficiary

Treated like an account change: the new details must be confirmed through an established channel, and the name must match — or the difference explained.

Always verified · name must match

/ Straight answers[ 07 / 07 ]

Plain questions. Honest limits.

Something else on your mind? Write to us from the contact block below.

Does Bayyinah tell me whether a request is fraud?+

No. It shows what the request says, where it differs from the details you hold, and what still has to be verified by a person. Language indicators and differences are reasons to check, not verdicts.

Does it make payments or connect to my bank?+

No. Bayyinah never moves money, authorizes transfers or connects to banking systems. An approval in Bayyinah records a decision within your workflow; the payment itself happens in your normal banking process.

Who can approve a bank account change?+

Only a member with the Reviewer role who did not create the case — and only after a person has recorded an independent verification with a “confirmed” outcome through a channel from your supplier reference.

What happens to the documents we upload?+

Files are stored privately and are visible only to members of your organization. When an AI provider is configured, the request text is sent to that provider for analysis; the case page names the provider before you run it. Retention is configurable per organization.

Can a document trick the AI into approving something?+

Embedded instructions are treated as data and flagged. More importantly, no tool available to the AI or the agent can approve, close, edit supplier details or contact anyone — those actions only exist for authenticated people with the right role.

Does it read scanned PDFs or images?+

Not in this version. Scanned or image-only PDFs are recorded as unreadable and you are asked to paste the text. Bayyinah never invents an extraction.

Can I use it in Arabic?+

Yes. The whole interface is available in Arabic and English — switch with the language button at the top of any page. Requests can be written in either language.
